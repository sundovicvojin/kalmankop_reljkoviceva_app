# Design System

## Direction

Dark architectural luxury: graphite, near-black, warm off-white, champagne gold, muted status colors i kontrolisan liquid glass.

## Tokens

- Background: near-black graphite
- Text: warm white
- Accent: champagne gold
- Available: muted emerald
- Reserved: warm amber
- Sold: graphite burgundy

## UI Rules

- Header, controls, hover card, drawer i bottom sheet mogu koristiti glass.
- Ne zatrpavati render karticama.
- Veliki radius samo za glavne povrsine, kontrolisane senke i malo vizuelnog suma.
- Mobile portrait mora biti potpuno upotrebljiv.
